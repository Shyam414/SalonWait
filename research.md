# SalonWait — Initial Research

## Problem (5 lines)

1. Kiran Saloon, located in Bollaram, Hyderabad, serves customers through walk-ins and bookings.
2. The person I want to help is Kiran, the owner of the salon.
3. The salon currently handles customer enquiries about service availability, waiting time and service costs.
4. Based on the initial information shared by the owner, the salon has approximately 5 customers on an average day, with customer volume increasing to around 15 on Saturdays and Sundays. These figures need to be clarified through observation.
5. I want to build a simple website where customers can view available services and their prices, check an estimated waiting time, and book a suitable time slot.

## Existing solution 1: Fresha

**Source:** Fresha — Salon Scheduling Software  
https://www.fresha.com/for-business/features/scheduling

**What it offers:**
- Fresha provides a scheduling calendar for managing appointments, staff and resources.
- It supports online appointment booking and waitlist management.
- Its waitlist features can help match customers with newly available appointment openings.

**Why a simpler solution may fit Kiran Saloon better:**
- Kiran Saloon is a small salon, so it may not need a complete salon-management platform.
- The owner is interested in a website focused on service information, booking, estimated waiting time and cost.
- The owner mentioned phone/internet access and cost as practical considerations. A lightweight website should therefore be easy to access and operate.

These points are initial considerations, not proof that Fresha would be unsuitable.

## Existing solution 2: Zenoti

**Source:** Zenoti — Salon Management Software  
https://www.zenoti.com/salon-management-software

**What it offers:**
- Zenoti provides an all-in-one salon-management platform with appointment booking, point of sale, client management and reporting.
- It includes digital appointment scheduling and staff-management features.
- It also provides waitlist and operational-management capabilities.

**Why a simpler solution may fit Kiran Saloon better:**
- Kiran Saloon is a small business and may not require all the features of a comprehensive management platform.
- The owner indicated that a separate application or complicated digital workflow could add extra work for the barber.
- A focused website could provide the required customer-facing features without introducing unnecessary management complexity.

The owner is open to using a website for bookings and estimated service time and cost, so the proposed solution should be designed around that requirement.

- **Weekly schedule:** Open six days per week; Tuesday is the weekly off day.

## Initial hypothesis

A simple salon website may help customers understand the available services, prices and expected waiting time before visiting. It may also help the owner organise bookings and manage customer arrivals.

The website could initially include:
1. A list of salon services and their prices.
2. Estimated service duration and waiting time.
3. Appointment or time-slot booking.
4. A simple owner-facing view of upcoming bookings.

Fieldwork will help determine which features are actually useful and how the salon currently manages its customers.

## Questions to investigate during fieldwork

1. How does Kiran currently manage walk-in customers and booked appointments?
2. How many barbers work at the salon?
3. What services are available, and what is the price of each?
4. How long does each service normally take?
5. How does the owner decide which customer is served next?
6. How does the owner estimate waiting time when a customer asks?
7. Does the salon experience delays when several customers arrive together?
8. Would customers prefer booking a specific time slot or joining a live queue?
9. Who would update service availability, prices and bookings on the website?
10. What should happen if a customer arrives late or a service takes longer than expected?

## Proposed direction

Build a lightweight website based on the salon's actual workflow. Use real service prices and observed service durations to calculate approximate costs and waiting times.

The estimates will be indicative rather than guaranteed because actual waiting times can change due to walk-ins, service delays, breaks and barber availability.
