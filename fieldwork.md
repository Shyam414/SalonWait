### Three Important Things the Owner Said

2. "Customers sometimes ask how long they need to wait before getting their haircut."
3. "A website where customers can check services, prices and book their slots would be useful."

### One Thing That Surprised Me

The salon has interest in a website that provides more than appointment booking. The owner is also interested in showing customers service prices and estimated waiting times before they arrive.

### What I Learned

The salon serves both walk-in customers and customers who book appointments. A useful website should consider both types of customers. It should also be simple enough for the owner to manage without adding unnecessary work.

## 5. Raw Data Collection Method

Service records were collected through direct observation at Kiran Saloon, with the owner's permission.

For each observed customer, the arrival time, service type, service start time, service completion time, anonymous barber ID and service status were recorded.

A phone clock was used to record timestamps. Customer names and phone numbers were not collected.

The collected records are stored in `data.csv`. Any missing or unavailable values are left blank rather than estimated.

**Collection period:** 17/11/2026 (9.00 AM to 11.00 AM)

**Note:** Only include records that were genuinely observed or obtained from the salon's actual records.


## 6. Findings and Project Changes

- **Main problem identified:** Customers may not know how long they need to wait before receiving a service.
- **What customers need:** Information about service availability, prices and estimated waiting time.
- **What the owner needs:** A simple way to manage appointments and communicate service availability.
- **Feature to prioritise:** Service listing, price display, appointment booking and estimated waiting time.
- **Feature to postpone:** Advanced payment integration and complex customer-management features.

## 7. Fieldwork Conclusion

The initial discussion suggests that a simple salon website could help customers view available services, understand prices and book a suitable time slot.

The proposed SalonWait system will use actual service durations and current queue information to calculate approximate waiting times. The estimate will depend on barber availability, existing bookings and customers ahead in the queue.

Further observation and genuine service records are required before finalising the waiting-time calculation and website workflow.
